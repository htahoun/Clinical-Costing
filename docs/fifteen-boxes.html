The Fifteen Boxes

Every dirham the clinic spends has to land in one of fifteen boxes. This is what each box means, in plain words, with the things we actually buy.

Vision Dental Clinic L.L.C Licence MF4159 DOH Cost Type list

One expense goes in one box. Never two.

If you can put an invoice in two boxes, it belongs in the one that describes what you bought, not who used it. When you genuinely cannot decide, ask Finance rather than guessing — a wrong box is harder to find later than an unanswered question.

The people we pay

Boxes 1–4

Every salary in the clinic sits in one of these four. The only question is what kind of job the person does — not which department pays them.

01
SWNursNurse salaries

Is this person a registered nurse?

Who goes here
  • Registered nurses

Watch out: only people who actually hold a nursing licence. Someone we call a "nurse" in day-to-day speech but who is really a chair-side helper belongs in box 3 or box 4.

02
SWDocDentists' salaries

Is this person a dentist?

Who goes here
  • Full-time dentists
  • Part-time dentists
  • Commissions paid to dentists

Don't forget commission. If a dentist earns a share of what they bill, that share belongs here too — not in Goods & Services. DOH calls this "Department #1", which for us simply means Dental; a hospital would have many.

03
SWAlliedClinical support salaries

Do they do clinical work, but they're not a dentist and not a nurse?

Who goes here
  • Dental assistants
  • Dental technicians
  • Radiology assistants
  • CSSD technician

CSSD is the sterilisation department — the person who cleans and sterilises our instruments.

04
SWNonClinAdmin & support salaries

Does this person never treat a patient?

Who goes here
  • Reception
  • Cashier
  • Insurance coordinator
  • Clinic manager
  • PRO
  • IT administrator
  • Finance manager
  • Marketing executive
  • Cleaner
  • Housekeeping
  • Driver

Easy way to remember: if the clinic were a shop instead of a clinic, these jobs would still exist.

What we use on patients

Boxes 5–10

Everything that reaches the patient — made for them, taken of them, given to them, or fitted into them.

05
LaboratoryLab work

Was it made or tested in a lab?

What goes here
  • Dental lab
  • Haematology lab
  • Biochemistry lab
  • Microbiology lab

For us this is overwhelmingly dental lab — crowns, bridges, dentures, aligners — whether made in-house or sent out. The medical labs are on the DOH list because hospitals use them.

06
ImagingX-rays & scans

Is it a picture of the inside of the patient?

What goes here
  • X-rays
  • OPG
  • CBCT
  • CT scan
  • Ultrasound
  • MRI
  • Mammography

Includes servicing and maintaining the imaging machines.

07
PharmacyMedicines

Is it a drug?

What goes here
  • Medicine cost
  • Pharmacy supplies
08
ProsthesesThings fitted into the patient

Does the patient walk out with it in their mouth?

What goes here
  • Dental implant screws
  • Abutments
  • Crown caps
  • Braces
  • Retainers
09
MSClinical consumables

Is it single-use, clinical, and binned at the end of the appointment?

What goes here
  • Gloves
  • Masks
  • Gowns
  • Caps
  • Shoe covers
  • Syringes
  • Needles
  • Drapes
  • Gauze
  • Cotton
  • Tapes
  • Blades
  • Sutures
  • Stitches
  • Antiseptics
  • IV sets
  • Oxygen masks
  • Test strips

"MS" stands for Medical & Surgical Supplies.

10
HotelFeeding & transporting patients

Did we feed the patient, or drive them?

What goes here for us
  • Patient nutrition
  • Juice & refreshments
  • Food
  • Patient car transport

The name is misleading. DOH wrote this box for hospitals, so their wording says bedsheets, gowns and TV. We have no beds, so that half doesn't apply — but the food and patient-transport half very much does. Keep this explanation with the audit file: a reviewer will ask why a clinic with no beds has a "Hotel" figure.

Running the clinic

Boxes 11–14

The cost of keeping the doors open, whether or not a single patient walks in.

11
GSGoods & services

Is it an office or admin running cost — not clinical, not a salary?

What goes here
  • Paper
  • Printer
  • Stationery
  • Uniforms
  • Furniture
  • IT hardware
  • Maintenance & repairs
  • Pest control
  • Tasneef audits
  • Consulting
  • Software subscriptions
  • Newspaper
  • Courier
  • Petrol
  • Vehicle renewal
  • Vehicle insurance
  • Vehicle maintenance
12
DepreciationWear and tear

Is this an asset losing value rather than money leaving the bank?

What goes here
  • Medical equipment
  • IT equipment
  • Furniture
  • Vehicles

We bought the dental chair once, but it wears out a little every year. That yearly slice is what goes here — no cash actually moves this month.

13
OHFFacility overheads

Would we still pay it on a day with zero patients?

What goes here
  • Electricity
  • Water
  • Internet
  • Building rent
  • Employee insurance renewal
  • Malpractice insurance renewal

OHF = Overhead Cost, Healthcare Facility. The cost of having a building at all.

14
OHCHead office & marketing

Is it promoting or supporting the business from above clinic level?

What goes here
  • Marketing campaigns
  • Website
  • Advertising
  • Training

OHC = Overhead Cost, Corporate Office.

The one that doesn't apply to us

Box 15
15
NABMsGovernment mandate adjustment

Government-funded insurance adjustments. Not applicable to us — leave at zero.

The full name is Negative Adjustment for Budgeted Mandate Services. It exists for facilities that receive government funding outside normal activity billing. We don't, so this box stays empty every month.

The four that get confused

These are the mix-ups that actually happen. Learn these four and the rest look after themselves.

Prostheses or MS?

A crown stays in the patient's mouth. The glove worn while fitting it goes in the bin.

Crown → Prostheses · Glove → MS

GS or Depreciation?

Furniture and IT hardware appear on both DOH lists. Our house rule: if we expense it in the year we buy it, it's GS. If we capitalise it and write it down over several years, only the yearly slice goes to Depreciation.

Never both — that double-counts the money

The clinic van, twice?

Yes, and that's correct. Petrol, insurance and servicing are running costs. The van losing value is wear and tear. Two different facts about the same vehicle.

Running → GS · Losing value → Depreciation

OHF or OHC?

OHF is the building — rent, electricity, water, insurance. OHC is everything that promotes or supports the business from above the clinic.

Building → OHF · Brand → OHC
