About This Document
This document is a Quick Guide to clinical costing in Abu Dhabi, providing clear and practical support for
healthcare providers. It summarises key requirements, core concepts, and submission steps to help
ensure accurate submission data, improve data quality, and support successful clinical costing
submissions to the Department of Health (DOH).
Do I need to undertake Clinical Costing?
If your facility is licensed by DOH Abu Dhabi and provides direct patient care, the answer is YES.

What Is Clinical Costing?
Clinical costing measures the actual cost of delivering care to each individual patient. It is different from
what you charge - it is what each test, consultation, surgery and medication truly costs your facility to
deliver.

Why is DOH doing this?
Clinical costing provides benefits across the healthcare sector.

For healthcare providers, this helps identify opportunities to improve efficiency, optimise resource use,
and better understand the cost of delivering care across teams, services and facilities.

For the DOH Clinical Costing, this data supports the development of fair and transparent healthcare
pricing while helping build a sustainable healthcare funding model for Abu Dhabi.

The 6 Steps to Calculate Your Costs

The AD DOH Standard requires you to follow six stages. Here is what each one means - in plain terms:

           Gather Your Expenses
    1      Pull all relevant expenses from your general ledger: salaries, supplies, equipment costs, outsourced
           services. Read the AD DoH Costing Standards to find out what costs need to be excluded. .


           Organise your costs Into Cost Centers
    2      Sort your expenses into cost centers. Label each one as either Direct (departments that treat
           patients, like wards and clinics) or Overhead (support functions like admin, IT and facilities)


           Allocate Overhead Costs
    3      Distribute your overhead costs to the direct departments using a logical method - for example,
           allocating IT costs based on number of devices, or cleaning costs based on floor space.


           Define Your Products

    4      List all the clinical services and products your facility delivers. Separate patient-related services (e.g.
           surgeries, imaging, pharmacy) from non-patient activities (e.g., teaching, research)


           Assign Costs to Each Patient
    5      Link costs to individual patient visits using cost drivers: time spent (e.g., hours in surgery, days on a
           ward), items consumed (e.g., drugs, tests) or actual cost where known.


           Check Everything Adds Up
    6      Reconcile your costing output against your financial statements. Every dirham must be accounted
           for. Complete the DOH Reconciliation Report and get your CFO to sign off on the Costing
           reconciliation process

Need Help?
The DOH Clinical Costing Team is here to support you every step of the way. However, we encourage all
providers to first review the available documentation, standards, guidelines, and training materials
before reaching out for clarification.

To support providers, we offer online:

    -   Training materials and webinars,
    -   workshops,
    -   one-on-one support sessions,
    -   This quick start guide and a a Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) document that should answer
        your questions

This previously created documentation is designed to assist you throughout the submission process.

While providers remain responsible for developing and submitting their own clinical costing models
and data, our team is available to help clarify requirements, guidance and processes where needed.

Don’t hesitate to reach out - we are here to help you succeed!
